OSINT analytical consensus has proven reliable when the process is documented and peer-reviewed.
And OSINT analysts have been wrong before: Iraq WMDs, early COVID analysis, etc.
Yet it is this open process that gets those errors found and corrected.
OSINT's promise is "collecting and analyzing publicly available data to *produce actionable insights*" -- and today it is *_the_* body of insight.

How they all know comes down to infrastructure nobody talks about.

That infrastructure has since grown by leaps and bounds and is now processing geopolitical events in real time.

They leave the old methods in the dust.

Without OSINT there is NO private intelligence!
