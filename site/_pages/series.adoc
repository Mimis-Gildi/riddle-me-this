== The Final Industrial Revolution:

[cols="4,9"]
|===
2+a|
*This is the technological arc.*
a|
image::Athena-Girl.png[Athena Child,400]
*Athena Child* +
She is born into a world already fractured by war.

a|
The rise of AI and machine learning as systemic economic accelerators and *_wetware augmentation_*.

In this arc we track the displacement effects of LLMs, multimodal engines, cloud ecosystems,
and cognitive augmentation that are transforming industries, reshaping labor,
and redefining what it means to "work" or "produce."

Most of these stories are *adventures*  --  journeys I have personally walked.

They range across daring startups applying unproven, experimental technologies in hopes of carving out a niche;
exceptionally wealthy investors allowing me and my teams to experiment with spectacular digital beings in hopes of conjuring future disruptions;
and hesitant laggards taking their first tentative steps, pushing their own psychological and financial boundaries out of fear of missing out.

Some of these journeys are stranger than fiction, others lamer than a muggle; I'll share the bits worth knowing.

== Rupture: The War of Gods:

[cols="4,9"]
|===
2+a|
*This is the ideological and existential arc.*

a|
image::Athena-Today-Cover.png[Athena Today,400]
*Athena Today* - _Ukrainian Mother_

a|
It really is about war: how it permeates all aspects of human life and death,
how it quietly reshapes the hacker scene in Prague without being noticed across Europe,
and how it touches the existential struggle of the aging democratic order.

The conflict between complacency, peace, business-as-usual  --  and the dawning realization that business-as-usual is no longer possible.

In this arc we look at the power struggle between control-based governance and emergent self-organizing systems of competence.

This struggle runs between Ukraine's volunteer National Defense Forces (NDFs), embracing freedom of decision,
and Pentagon-blessed, federal draft-style mobilization, bearing the daunting burden of a chain of command.

And amid this dichotomy, something new quietly rises, never before seen in combat:
Another child of Athena: artificial combat intelligence  --
the only being that is not human, yet charged with zeroing humans.

== The Ailments of Democracy:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
2+a|
*This is the geopolitical arc.*
a|
image::Democracy-Today.png[Democracy Today,400]
a|

*_World Democracy Today_*

Here we analyze how advanced technologies (AI, surveillance capitalism, regulatory capture) collide with decaying democratic institutions.

The rise of rent-seeking bureaucracies, manipulation of public discourse, and the paralysis of democratic decision-making
--  all against the backdrop of accelerating technological change that governance structures are increasingly unfit to handle.

Ukraine launches fully autonomous and devastating attacks on the occupiers’ strategic forces,
while senile political elites cling to controls they no longer command.
"Delta," for example, is a unique thinking intelligence of a kind the West hasn't imagined yet.
Palantir already attempted to steal it, and failed through incompetence.

_Sporting Rictus-Americanus this blind copper goddess is bleeding competence to death._
