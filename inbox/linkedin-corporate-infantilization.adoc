What does a candidate learn about your company from ONE interview question?

Your interviewer asked me:

"You get too much data in your React single page application. How to load it?"

They thought they were sizing me up. But an interview is a two-way street --
and that question read me your architecture diagram out loud. Sticky notes and all.

In a disciplined shop this question cannot be asked -- it has no meaning there.
The aggregate decides HOW MUCH to send, and that is all the read model will ever see.
Where the question is normal, nobody owns the behavior: data hoses in from "somewhere,"
and the one component that can't push back gets ordered to cope. That is not a React
problem. That is Corporate Infantilization -- and your best candidates can smell it
from a single question.

Two ArchiMate diagrams in the article: the story a disciplined org tells itself,
and the sticky-note version -- side by side.
